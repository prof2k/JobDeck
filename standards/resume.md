# Resume standard

The resume step is a quality and evidence check, not an automatic career-choice
engine.

This standard evolves when a reusable resume-review or document-quality rule
improves. Candidate facts and requested positioning remain under `private/`.

## Review

Look for:

- unsupported or internally inconsistent claims;
- unclear timelines, titles, ownership, or outcomes;
- dense wording, weak hierarchy, and unnecessary repetition;
- ATS-hostile layout, graphics, columns, headers, or contact information;
- important context that can be clarified with a factual question.

Distinguish facts already in the resume, facts verified in artifacts, facts the
candidate confirmed, and unknowns. Never turn an inference into a resume fact.

## Revision

Preserve the original under `private/resume/original/`. When the user requests new files, make an editable DOCX
the maintained source and derive the PDF from it. Use black text, conventional
section headings, ordinary bullets, selectable text, and restrained spacing.
Prefer a readable two-page resume to a compressed one-page document.

Render and visually inspect every generated document. Confirm there is no
clipping, overlap, broken glyph, orphaned heading, or nearly empty final page.
